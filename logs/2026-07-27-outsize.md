## Data (principle #7)
scripts/gen-geodata.mjs fetches Natural Earth 1:50m Admin-0 (public domain) and bundles name/continent/centroid/rings/population(POP_EST)/area. Area is computed by spherical-excess integration over the RAW outline (50m chosen because 110m undercounts Saudi Arabia's crinkly coast by ~10%); validated against 53 official figures within 8%, with Saudi Arabia's disputed-border area overridden to the official 2,149,690 (NE draws ~1.93M). Adaptive silhouette grid (fine for small countries, coarse for giants) + 2dp rounding → ~105KB gz. Sub-national/disputed entities (Hong Kong, Kosovo, Puerto Rico, …) bundled but never a card.
