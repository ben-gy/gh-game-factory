## Game Details
- **Name / repo:** Squirm / ben-gy/squirm
- **Genre:** card (bluffing / party)
- **Core loop:** slide a card face-down + declare a critter (truth or lie); receiver
  believes / calls bluff / peeks & passes; wrong guesser keeps it; 4-of-a-kind (3 in
  Swarm) = out; last un-buried wins.
- **Multiplayer:** live P2P 2–6, **host-authoritative with genuine hidden info** (the
  fleet's first — recent P2P games were same-seed races). Solo-vs-3-bots is the
  instant single-player surface.
- **Stack / render:** vanilla-ts / DOM+CSS (procedural SVG critters, Web Audio SFX)
- **Engine modules:** net, rematch, turn, lobby (+ qr), rng, sound, storage, mobile

### The balance sim overruled the design FOUR times (built first, refereed the tuning)
1. **always-peek dominated** (53% parlour / 32% swarm) — peek was a free escape from
   the lethal card. Fixed with a **per-round peek budget** (a spendable resource).
2. **seat-0 win bias at 4p** — really the **ranking tie-break on seat index** (the
   ballast "ties → seat 0" lesson). Fixed with a **seed-derived tie key**.
3. **90% forfeit rate in 4p Snap** — the "collect N" mechanic barely fired because a
   fixed deck went thin at higher player counts. Fixed by **scaling copies with
   player count**; forfeits → ~0% everywhere.
4. **"spider death-sentence"** (33% of losses) — a **measurement artifact** in the
   test's byType tie-break on forfeits; the true kill-type (eliminate event) is
   uniform. Also randomised the first giver from the seed.

Final: every seat within ~5 of chance at 3p/4p, no rote script beats the balanced
reader (bluff-always 11–16%), losses even across critters, all games terminate.
