## Game Details
- **Name / repo:** Waft / ben-gy/waft
- **Genre:** arcade
- **Core loop:** fireflies bloom in a grid over a faint mirrored camera view; wave your hand
  (per-zone frame-difference motion) over one to catch it; clean catches build a combo, a whiff
  or a wasp (Sting) breaks it.
- **Multiplayer:** live P2P, 2–6, parallel same-seed race (no game state on the wire; never a
  camera image) + async-seed + UTC daily.
- **Stack / render:** vanilla-ts / canvas.
- **Engine modules used:** net, rematch, turn, lobby (+qr), rng, sound, storage, mobile, identity.
- **Primary input:** camera motion; fallback tap/click/number-keys.

## Juice
Custom procedural SFX (catch chime pitched by combo, soft miss blip, harsh sting buzz),
gold catch-particle bursts + red sting shards, screen shake scaling with combo (bigger on a
sting), firefly scale-in/bob/fade tweens, a cyan motion-pulse ring per zone so the player SEES
their wave land, and a popping combo counter. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.

## Errors & Resolutions
- **Balance sim caught a non-load-bearing mechanic** (the whole point of #18): a random masher
  scored identically to a perfect reader because sparse zones + long firefly life + a per-zone
  cooldown made random swatting catch everything and max the combo. Fixed by making a whiff break
  the combo (precision is now the skill). Mutation-verified both directions.
- **Camera source ownsStream param:** the session reuses one camera stream across rounds, so the
  per-round camera source must NOT stop the shared stream on dispose; added `ownsStream=false` and
  stop the stream only at the menu/leave/unload (verified: track live→ended on quit).
- **Pane limitations (not game bugs):** the browser pane throttles the rAF game loop and does not
  deliver key events, so gameplay/input was driven and verified through the synchronous `stepFor`
  hook and real pointer clicks; the accumulated catch-particles in screenshots are a
  throttled-then-flushed rendering artifact (they decay in real-time play).
