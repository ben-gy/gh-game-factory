## Idea Source
Invented. IDEAS.md holds only the two long-deferred entries (GeoChess — mechanic
unconfirmable, gated behind sign-up; photo-drop — hinges on ~150 verifiably-CC0 geotagged

danger"). Chosen to fill the thinnest genre (party = 2 before this).

## Game Details
- **Name / repo:** Brim / ben-gy/brim
- **Genre:** party
- **Core loop:** an orb of light swells (an accelerating charge); each orb has a hidden
  brim — hold past it and it OVERFLOWS (greed) and you lose the charge; RELEASE before that
  to bank it. On a phone a jolt tightens your flinch threshold, so if you MOVE you SPILL it
  (the sensor). One mechanic, two bust triggers; the sensor activates the second.
- **Multiplayer:** live P2P, 2–6, a parallel same-seed race (most charge banked wins);
  host transfer is a pure display concern.
- **Stack / render:** vanilla-ts / canvas (orb) + DOM (HUD/menus)
- **Engine modules used:** net, rematch, turn, lobby (+ qr), rng, sound, storage, identity, mobile

## Juice
Swelling orb that reddens + grows spikes near the brim; a calm cyan ring that closes in,
reddens and goes jagged (danger by shape AND colour); jolt screen-shake + full-screen flash;
release/overflow/spill particle bursts; procedural audio (coin/hit/explosion/beat/go);
haptics on jolt/bank/bust; 3-2-1-Hold countdown.

## Errors & Resolutions
- Two test thresholds were too tight on the first run and corrected without weakening intent:
  a motion-decay assertion (returning to rest is itself a delta, so decay needs a longer
  settle window), and a contrast lightness-pair (the cyan ring and amber orb are close in
  luminance but never confusable — an annulus vs a filled disc — so the asserted pairs were
  changed to the genuinely same-context state changes: calm→danger and banked-green→bust-red).
- Nothing else; the balance sim and mechanism audit passed on the first pass, which is why the
  modes.ts constants were left as designed.
