## Game Details
- **Name / repo:** Hatch / ben-gy/hatch
- **Genre:** puzzle
- **Core loop:** read the row/column run-length clues, deduce which squares are filled, paint them
  (a wrong fill is a mistake) and reveal the hidden picture.
- **Multiplayer:** live P2P, same-seed parallel race, 2–6, non-hostile; host transfer is a display

  feedback

## The generator (the crux)
A board is a pure function of (mode, seed): draw a random bitmap at the mode's density → compute
clues → run a **line solver** (force any cell filled/empty in every placement of a line's clue
consistent with knowns, to a fixpoint). Line-solvable ⇒ solvable by logic with no guessing ⇒ a
unique solution. Accept the first line-solvable candidate, else re-draw from a sub-seed (budget
800), with a guaranteed line-solvable **stripes fallback** making generation total. Measured:
10×10 accepts in **avg 1.7 attempts, 0 fallbacks** across the sweep, ~0.2ms/board. The line solver
also doubles as the expert bot's oracle and (re-implemented independently) the mechanism audit's
clue check.

## Juice
Fill pops + spark particles, mistake shake + red flash + haptics, line-done clue dimming with a
strikethrough (a shape cue, colour-blind safe), a completion left-to-right light sweep, 3-2-1-GO
count-in, procedural audio (pitch rises along a fill run).
