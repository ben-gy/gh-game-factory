## Game Details
- **Name / repo:** Cairn / ben-gy/cairn
- **Genre:** card
- **Core loop:** take an exposed stone one rune (±1, wrap A↔K) from your hand → it becomes the hand
  and uncovers stones beneath; chains build a heat multiplier; draw from the scree (resets heat,
  spends a limited budget) when stuck; clear the cairn.
- **Multiplayer:** live P2P, parallel same-seed race, 2–6.
- **Stack / render:** vanilla-ts / canvas.
- **Engine modules used:** net, rematch, turn, lobby, rng, sound, storage, identity, mobile.

## The generator (the crux)
Pure fn of (mode, seed, index), **solvable by construction**: pyramid layout → seeded valid removal
order (a pyramid always has an exposed stone → totality) → assign runes as a ±1 walk (first stone
chains off the flip so an opening move always exists; later stones chain or draw-then-take). The walk
IS a stored full-clear solution; D=solution draws sets drawBudget=D+slack. Scree's small slack is the
sudden-death lever; Ramble's large slack makes running out a soft end, never a loss.

## Juice
Fly-to-hand animation, chain pops that grow with heat, rising coin pitch, draw slide + heat snap-back,
green clear-flash + burst, coral bury-shake + haptic, amber legal-ring + lift, shadow scrim on covered
stones. Procedural SFX, prefers-reduced-motion aware, capped particle/flyer pools.

## Errors & Resolutions
- **Mid-round Quit → blank lobby.** The engine renders nothing for a peer still `seated` in a
  `playing` round ("the game owns the screen", lobby.ts). The in-game Quit showed the lobby while
  still seated → blank. Fix: Quit now calls `rounds.finish()` first (phase playing→waiting) so the
  lobby renders. Verified live (Back-to-lobby from results renders cleanly after the fix).
- **generator.test cascade assertion** — asserted `s.draws === solDraws` after the loop, but a
  Cascade clear loads the next cairn and resets draws. Captured the first cairn's numbers before the
  run and gated the draw-count assertion to single-cairn modes.
