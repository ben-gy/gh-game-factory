## Juice
Pitched `blip` per card (pitch varies with the card, so a trick has a little melody), `thud` on trick
resolution, `win`/`lose` on a task landing or dying, high blip on a signal, `select` on a draft pick.
Trick zone pulses on resolution; haptics on task resolution; 3-2-1-Go count-in with audio. All of it
respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## What the simulation changed (principle #18 — build the sim FIRST, let it referee)
1. **A forced draft is a dice roll.** Dealing exactly as many task cards as the mission needs gave
   mission 1 a 33% win rate at four players — and **0 wins from 11** when the card landed in its own
   owner's hand, because a card you hold must win a trick *as itself* and no trump can help you.
   Fixed with spare candidates **and** the own-hand draft rule. The second is the better idea: it
   makes every task an act of trust.
2. **The bot hoarded other people's task cards** rather than risk them, so the colour never came out
   and the mission died on the last trick when it was finally forced. Teaching it to hand the card
   over *when the owner is winning and nothing compulsory can beat them* took mission 1 from 50% to
   85%. The distinction matters: only following suit is compulsory, so a crewmate winning with the
   top card of the led suit is safe, and one winning with a low trump is not.
3. **A ladder that adds a task every other rung is a cliff** (81% → 40%). Slowed it, and put the
   chain-link schedule deliberately out of phase with the task schedule so no rung ever adds both.
4. **What the sim refused to endorse:** an A/B over 400 identical seeds per cell showed the signal
   system moves a **bot** crew's win rate by **<1pp** — inside noise. Not tuned away: identical bots
   already coordinate implicitly, and a comms channel only carries information when the other minds
   are opaque. Reported as a limit of the sim, not as a property of the game.

## Bugs found during verification (all mutation-verified)
1. **Draft desync.** Skipping a seat that cannot claim anything depends on that seat's *hidden hand*,
   so host and guest computed different draft turns; the guest rejected the host's log, asked to be
   re-dealt, and the two traded messages forever. The skip is now an explicit logged action (A_PASS).
2. **Authority hole.** The host executed whatever seat a peer packed into its action — one crewmate
   could have played everybody's cards. Now checked against the sender; had no test, so I wrote one.
3. **Frozen mission after takeover.** A seat whose hand nobody attested had nobody driving it: board
   correct, nothing thrown, simply nobody's turn forever.
4. **Stale results screen.** The end-of-mission delay was not cancellable, so starting a new mission
   inside it showed the old summary against the new game — seen on screen as "Mission 1 failed ·
   0 of 0 tasks landed" beside a button offering "Mission 2".
5. **Suit inks all at one luminance.** Picked by eye, all five landed within 1.02:1 of each other —
   five hues of one grey, identical in greyscale. Re-solved onto a 1.159-step ladder.
6. **Bots spent both signals on the same card** (visible on screen as "highest Drape" twice).
7. **Unbounded re-deal retry** after a lost attestation; now bounded, and the peer spectates.
